# Contributing

This is a pattern, not a product — improvements to the **method** are very welcome.

Good contributions:
- New or sharper **lint checks** (see `docs/accuracy.md`) — ideally with a one-paragraph rationale and a failure example.
- **Token-budget** refinements (`docs/token-budget.md`).
- Adapters/notes for running the protocol on other agents or editors.
- Clearer templates.

Please **do not** contribute real domain content — keep examples to short, invented placeholders (see `docs/principles.md`, "separate the layers", and the repo's Privacy note).

Open an issue to discuss anything substantial before a PR. Keep prose tight and concrete.
